a) Data stored on your device

Game progress and preferences are saved locally on your device (browser local storage). This data stays on the device and is removed when you clear the App’s data or uninstall it. If you choose a profile picture, you pick a single image with the system picker (no photo-library permission is requested); the App crops it to a small 256×256 copy that is stored only on your device and is never uploaded or shared. You can remove it at any time by selecting “Default”. Your game archive (the moves and results of your last 200 finished games) is also stored only on your device; you can delete any game from the archive screen. You can create a manual backup file from the in-app “Back up progress” screen; that file is created and controlled entirely by you and is not sent to us.

Android backup: the App saves a copy of your game progress in its private storage so that Android’s built-in backup can store it in your own Google account and restore it when you install the App on a new phone. This backup is handled by Google under your device’s backup settings (Settings → Google → Backup); we cannot access it. Turning off device backup stops it. Home-screen widget and sharing: the widget shows your streak and daily-puzzle status using data stored on the device only. Images you share (for example a weekly report card) are created on the device and sent only to the app you pick in the system share menu.

g) World Ranking (only when online features are switched on)

The yearly World Ranking is not active yet; it starts only when the App's online features are opened. Until then your yearly XP is counted on your device only. Once active, the App sends our server (Google Firebase, region europe-west1) your player name, your country if you chose one (a two-letter code such as “TR”), the XP you earned this year, your total XP, your ELO and the date you first started playing, linked to an anonymous account ID. Other players can see a public list of the top 1,000 entries containing only the player name, country flag (if chosen), yearly level and ELO (never your account ID). You can turn on “Hide my name in the list” to appear as “Hidden Player”; your country is then not shown either, and your position still counts. Country is optional (“Prefer not to say” is the default) and can be changed or removed at any time in your profile. The optional gender field in your profile is stored only on your device and is never sent to us or shown to others. Names that look inappropriate or contain personal details (links, phone numbers) are shown as “Player #XXXX”. Please do not use your real full name as your player name. At the end of each year the top 10,000 players receive a permanent title (e.g. “2026 World #1”) stored with their account. Ranking data is kept for the season plus the following year; titles are kept as long as your account exists, and you can ask us to delete them.
